Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fiscal Edge ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects personal information provided through this website (www.fiscal-edge.org). We are committed to handling your information responsibly and in line with applicable professional and legal requirements.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you choose to provide when you contact us, including:

  • Your name and company name
  • Your email address and phone number
  • The service or area of interest you select
  • The contents of any message you send us

We may also collect limited technical information automatically, such as browser type, device type, and pages visited, to help us understand how the website is used and keep it secure.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and requests
  • Discuss potential professional engagements
  • Provide information about our services when you ask for it
  • Meet our legal, regulatory, and professional obligations

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Cookies and Analytics

This website may use cookies or similar technologies to support basic functionality and to understand general usage patterns. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings; doing so may affect some parts of the site.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not disclose your personal information to third parties except where necessary to respond to your inquiry, where required by law or regulation, or where you have consented. Information you share in connection with a potential professional engagement is treated as confidential.

5. Data Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by applicable law and professional record-keeping standards.

7. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below.

8. Third-Party Links

This website may contain links to third-party websites (for example, our contact form may open WhatsApp or your email application). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties, and we encourage you to review their policies.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact us at info@fiscal-edge.org or +20 1271360003.
